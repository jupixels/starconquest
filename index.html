Star Frontier

Welcome, pilot

Your goal: grow from a tiny fighter into the best-equipped pilot in the galaxy.

You fly a small spaceship on a big frontier. Here is how it works:

  • Mine and fight. Shoot glowing asteroids for ore, and shoot drones for salvage.
  • Sell. Dock at a space station and sell what you collected for credits.
  • Upgrade. Spend credits on upgrades, weapons and bigger ships.
  • Explore. Jump to other star systems. The deeper you go, the tougher the drones and the richer the rewards.

Story unlocks: once you travel to another star system, a story campaign opens up. You can start it whenever you like.

This short tutorial shows you the basics and takes about two minutes.
